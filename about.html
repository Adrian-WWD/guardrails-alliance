Who we are

A coalition that refuses to look the other way

The AI Guardrails Alliance is made up of workers, organizers, policy experts, and everyday people who believe that the most powerful technology ever built shouldn't be controlled by a handful of unaccountable billionaires — and that our democracy shouldn't be for sale to the highest bidder.

We are not anti-technology. We believe AI can be transformative, beneficial, and genuinely good for people. But that future doesn't happen by accident — it requires rules, accountability, and a government that actually represents the public, not just its donors.

We were formed in direct response to Leading the Future — a $125 million super PAC bankrolled almost entirely by five Silicon Valley figures — and the broader campaign to elect politicians who will gut AI oversight before it can take root.

The question isn't whether AI will change the world. It's whether the people affected by that change will have any say in how.
— AI Guardrails Alliance

Our mission

Put guardrails on AI before it's too late to matter

We exist to do three things: expose the money behind the deregulation push, elect candidates who will stand up for AI accountability, and build a broad public coalition that can outlast any single election cycle.

What we believe

The principles behind everything we do:

AI must be accountable. Systems that affect jobs, healthcare, housing, and civil rights need oversight, transparency, and legal accountability — not blanket immunity.
Democracy isn't for sale. No individual or company — however rich — should be able to purchase a regulatory environment that protects their profits at the expense of the public.
Workers come first. The people whose jobs are being displaced by AI deserve a seat at the table — and a safety net that actually catches them.
Children must be protected. AI-generated abuse, manipulative algorithms, and addictive systems designed to exploit kids are not acceptable byproducts of innovation — they are failures of governance.
Communities have rights. Massive data centers that consume local water and power without consent are not progress — they are extraction. Affected communities deserve a voice.
The road forward benefits everyone. We don't have to choose between technological progress and human dignity. The guardrails we're fighting for make AI better — not just safer.
